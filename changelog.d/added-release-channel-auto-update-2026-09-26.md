- Managed installs (the clone at `~/.ccc/claude-command-center`) now follow releases instead of `main`: launching, restarting and the "Update available" pill fast-forward to the newest `vX.Y.Z` tag, never downgrading or resetting. Dev clones and symlinked installs keep tracking `origin/main`; override with `CCC_UPDATE_CHANNEL=release|main`. An hourly idle auto-update for managed installs is included but off by default (`CCC_AUTO_UPDATE=1` to opt in); `/api/version/check` gains an `update_channel` field.
