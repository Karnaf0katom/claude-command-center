- New sessions pick their folder from the prompt. A path or repo name in your message is matched locally, always, with no network. Add an optional TypeSafe Jev key under Settings > BYOK for smarter matching when the message never names the project. Prompts are scrubbed of likely secrets and are only sent when a Jev key is set. Confident picks show an "Auto-picked <folder> · undo" chip; less certain ones show a "<folder>?" chip you can click.
