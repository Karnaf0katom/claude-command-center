- **Jobs tab**: a new sidebar tab next to Workers lists scheduled jobs with their outcomes: the Hermes VM's own systemd timers (7-day pass/fail history, last run, duration, next run, and the job's last output line or an explicit `CCC_OUTCOME:` line) plus the laptop launchd agents that are actually scheduled. Failed and stale jobs sort first; new `GET /api/jobs` serves it from a background-refreshed cache.
